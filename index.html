02 / The human in the loop

I used to route packets.
Now I follow questions.

I’m Kristian. My starting point was network engineering: packet routing, sockets, and the architecture that keeps machines talking.

That led to a broader fascination with how systems behave—how agents learn, how people cooperate, and how simple rules become complicated worlds.

These days, I’m exploring AI, sociology, game theory, and applied mathematics by building experiments. Sometimes useful. Sometimes absurd. Ideally both.
